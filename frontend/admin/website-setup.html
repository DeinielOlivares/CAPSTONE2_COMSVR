    Website Setup

    Website Branding

    The brand shown in the navbar/sidebar across the Citizen, Staff and Admin portals. Takes effect right away everywhere, but doesn't change the browser tab's title.

    No custom logo

    Recommended: a square image, ideally a PNG with a transparent background. JPG or WebP also work, up to 5 MB. Leave unset to keep the default leaf icon.

    Anything after a | is shown on a smaller line under the name, e.g. "Barangay Platero | Ordinance Violation Reporting and Monitoring System".

    Site Footer

    The text and links at the bottom of every citizen page. The contact details and emergency hotline above also appear there. Leave a list empty to use the built-in links.

    Quick Links
    Legal

    A link can be a page name such as faqs.html, or a full address starting with https://, mailto: or tel:.

    Edit Legal Page

    Leave a blank line between blocks. Use - item for a bullet, 1. item for a numbered item, | cell | cell | for a table row (first row is the header), **bold**, [link text](faqs.html), and {{address}}, {{email}}, {{phone}} or {{name}} from Barangay Details.
