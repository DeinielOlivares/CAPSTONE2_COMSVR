      Terms & Conditions and Data Privacy Notice

      Please read this notice in full before creating a Barangay Platero OVRMS account. Scroll to the bottom to enable "I Agree."

      1. Acceptance of Terms

      By creating an account on Barangay Platero OVRMS, you agree to use the system only for filing genuine ordinance-violation reports, concerns, and suggestions within the barangay it serves. Submitting a false or malicious report is a violation of these Terms and may be referred to the barangay for appropriate action, including penalties under applicable local ordinances.

      2. Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)

      Barangay Platero OVRMS collects and processes personal information — including your name, contact number, address, email, and any photos or videos you submit as evidence — strictly in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. We process this information only for the purposes of verifying your identity, managing your account, and handling the reports, concerns, and suggestions you submit.

      3. What We Collect

      Account details you provide at signup (name, email, phone number, address), the contents of any report, concern, or suggestion you file, any photo/video evidence you attach, and basic activity logs (such as login times) used for account security and audit purposes.

      4. How We Use Your Data

      Your information is used to verify that reports are filed by a real, accountable resident; to route your reports and concerns to the appropriate Barangay Staff for review and action; to notify you by email about updates to your reports, concerns, and suggestions; and to maintain an audit trail for accountability.

      5. Data Sharing

      Your personal information is only shared internally with authorized Barangay Staff and Administrators responsible for reviewing and acting on your submissions. Barangay Platero OVRMS does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or any other unrelated purpose.

      6. Your Rights

      Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to erasure or blocking, to damages, to file a complaint, and to data portability regarding your personal information. You may contact the Barangay through the system's support channels to exercise any of these rights.

      7. Data Retention and Security

      Your data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes above, or as required by law, and is protected using reasonable organizational and technical safeguards against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

      8. Accountability for False Reports

      Filing a report you know to be false or misleading is subject to penalty under local ordinances. By proceeding, you acknowledge and accept this responsibility.

      By clicking "I Agree" below, you confirm that you have read and understood this notice and consent to the collection and processing of your personal data as described above.
